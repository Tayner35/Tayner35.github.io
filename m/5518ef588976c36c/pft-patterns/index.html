How to interpret the table

Obstruction

Defined by a low FEV₁/FVC ratio. RV may rise from air trapping, while TLC may remain normal or increase.

Restriction

A low FVC only suggests restriction. A low TLC is required to confirm it.

DLCO localization

Low DLCO with restriction favors intrinsic parenchymal disease; preserved DLCO favors an extraparenchymal cause.

Scope

These are typical Exam 1 patterns, not absolute diagnostic rules. Severity, treatment, effort, and mixed disease can change individual values.
